## 👋 About Me

I'm a **data analyst and physics researcher** interested in turning complex datasets into reliable analysis, interpretable models, and practical decisions.

My background in solar physics and quantitative research shaped how I approach data: question assumptions, investigate anomalies, validate results, quantify uncertainty, and make conclusions reproducible.

I'm applying that same scientific discipline to business and customer analytics, machine learning, and data engineering.

- 🔎 **Analytics:** Explore data, investigate business questions, and communicate findings.
- 🐍 **Programming:** Use Python, Pandas, NumPy, and SQL to transform and analyze data.
- 📊 **Business intelligence:** Build KPI-driven analyses, dashboards, and reporting workflows.
- 🤖 **Machine learning:** Develop predictive models and investigate their performance and limitations.
- ☀️ **Scientific computing:** Apply statistical analysis and ML to solar active-region research.
- 🛠️ **Engineering mindset:** Work toward reliable pipelines, automated checks, and reproducible projects.

> **My principle:** A result is only as useful as the data, assumptions, validation, and reasoning behind it.
